# Darkinsider website copy

## Brand
Darkinsider is the freelance film brand of Illia Vereshchuk, a director,
cinematographer and editor based in Munich. Focus: automotive films,
short films, image films. The website is an English-language portfolio,
not a service catalogue: the work speaks first, the text supports it.
Audience: mainly private clients, often with their own companies.

## Voice
- Cinematic, calm, confident. Premium and minimal, like the design.
- Talk about images, light, rhythm and story, not "content" or "reach".
- Short sentences. Lots of air. Every word must earn its place.
- First person ("I"), direct and personal, never corporate "we".
- No marketing clichés: avoid "passionate", "high-quality", "tailored
  solutions", "bring your vision to life", "elevate", "stunning".
- No exclamation marks, no emojis.

## Formats
- Headlines: 2–6 words.
- Project descriptions: 1–3 sentences: what the film is, the visual idea,
  Illia's role (direction / camera / edit).
- About page: max ~120 words, focused on how he sees and builds images.
- CTAs: quiet and clear, e.g. "Start a project", "Get in touch".
- Write in natural British or American English consistently (choose
  American unless told otherwise); proofread spelling and grammar.

## Process
1. Before writing, check which page and section the text is for.
2. Offer 2–3 options for headlines and key lines.
3. Never invent clients, awards, numbers or projects. If facts are
   missing, ask.
